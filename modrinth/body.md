# Dynamic Skateboards

Early alpha arcade-style skateboarding for NeoForge 1.21.1.

## What's in it

- A craftable skateboard: hold it in the main hand to skate.
- Charge-and-release ollies, forgiving air control, flips, grabs, and manuals.
- Geometry-derived straight and curved grinds on the included cobblestone skatepark kit.
- Bails that drop a physical skateboard, recoverable by walking over it.

Dynamic Skateboards requires [Sable](https://modrinth.com/mod/sable) at runtime.

Source and issues: https://github.com/brooswit-minecraft/dynamic-skateboards
